The draft Dragon’s Gold composite gradient retains Arcturus / Rick Fleuren / Miniature Painter Pro’s #CDA24E as its main colour and bright stop, and credits GERInFact for #4F442C as its dark stop. The official Trans Atlantis Games / Duncan Rhodes Painting Academy PDF is reference evidence for the illustrated tonal range; it is not the source of the numeric endpoints or a manufacturer-specified gradient.
